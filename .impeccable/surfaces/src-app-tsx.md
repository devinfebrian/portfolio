# Surface: app

## Scope and mode

Primary route: the single-page portfolio at `/`. Mode: Experience. Audience: engineering hiring managers, collaborators, and clients. Job: judge Devin Febrian's work and reach him. Action: open an exhibit, open a live build, or make contact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a live broadsheet edition, not a themed landing page: Devin's real work is reported as front-page news, and the page refuses the standard hero-plus-card-grid shell.

OWN-WORLD: Newsprint cream sheets on a warm ink desk, black ink type, one oxblood red used for stamps, folios, rules, and a single front-page action. Besley is the masthead and headline voice, Newsreader the reading voice, Courier Prime the newspaper's typewritten metadata, Archivo Narrow the technical/tag voice. Sheets are ruled and folded, never rounded cards; the printing desk and floating paper are the only scene.

STORY: The visitor understands that Devin builds fullstack systems with an AI/ML edge, believes it because the three real projects and live builds are laid out as reported stories with moving photographs, and acts by opening a project or writing to the editor.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed morning edition. A slim fixed paper strip carries the paper mark, section tabs, and press-sound switch. The broadsheet opens with a giant centred "THE FEBRIAN PROPHET" masthead over edition rules; below, a three-column lead story gives Devin's name, role, a truthful deck, and a red "TURN TO THE PORTFOLIO" action, beside a sepia wizard-photograph plate of the Sentinel capture and "Inside this edition" contents. The printing desk and ink motes show only at the paper's outer margins.

FORM: Broadsheet front page with a living-photograph desk, pinned by the user's brief. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
