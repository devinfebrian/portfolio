# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Bun with React, Vite, and React Three Fiber for 3D/animation (user-selected). Single-page, statically deployable build.

## Users

Prospective employers, clients, and collaborators evaluating Devin Febrian for fullstack engineering work with an AI/ML focus. They arrive from GitHub, LinkedIn, or a shared link, often on a laptop and sometimes on a phone, with a short window to judge capability, taste, and fit.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Devin Febrian that presents his work, engineering range, and contact path. Success means a visitor remembers the work, understands what he builds, and reaches out or opens a project.

## Positioning

Fullstack engineering with a machine-learning and LLM focus, backed by a public body of shipped repositories. The specific claim set remains open until real project copy is supplied; no performance numbers, customer names, or capabilities will be invented.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a single scrolling surface, open project links, and look for a resume or contact route. The site competes for attention with ordinary developer portfolios and should make a strong first impression quickly while staying usable on small screens.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single chapter-style scrolling experience.
- React Three Fiber atmospheric scene and motion.
- Typefaces: Besley for masthead and headlines, Newsreader for body reading, Courier Prime for typewritten metadata, Archivo Narrow for technical labels. All are self-hosted through Fontsource.
- Real bio, project details, and contact links are supplied or drawn from public repositories and live deployments. The owner should confirm role attribution and any outcome claims before the site is used for applications.
- No backend or CMS is required; the site is a static build.
- Sound and heavy motion stay opt-in or user-controlled.

## Brand Commitments

- Wordmark and title-card name: Devin Febrian.
- Visual world requested: a wizarding newspaper in the spirit of the Daily Prophet — drenched newsprint, black ink, one red editorial accent, moving photographs, and folding-paper page motion. Full tone, not a neutral site with a newspaper header.
- Typography roles are binding: Besley (display), Newsreader (reading), Courier Prime (metadata), Archivo Narrow (technical labels).
- Do not use franchise logos, character art, or other protected assets; this is an original homage, not a replica.

## Product Principles

1. Show the engineering, do not assert it: real repositories and specifics outrank adjectives.
2. Atmosphere serves navigation: newspaper composition must never make the work or contact path harder to find.
3. One authored world, not a skin: navigation, project cards, and states all belong to the same visual language.
4. Motion is material, not decoration: it rewards attention and yields to reduced-motion and low-power settings.
5. Leave obvious slots for Devin's real copy, resume, and links instead of inventing evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; keep body text at WCAG AA contrast against the newsprint palette; full keyboard navigation and visible focus; audio off by default with an explicit control; decorative 3D hidden from assistive technology.
